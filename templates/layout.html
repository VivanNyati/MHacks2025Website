Who We Are

Climate Transparency Initiative is a team of passionate researchers and developers committed to shining light on worldwide climate issues. By automatically gathering up-to-date climate data from all across the web, we aim to keep the public well-informed and inspired to act.

Our Goals

  • Aggregate accurate, up-to-date climate data from global sources.
  • Increase transparency and access to environmental information.
  • Mobilize communities toward sustainable, science-driven action.
  • Empower individuals and organizations to make informed decisions.
